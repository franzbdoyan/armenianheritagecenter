New Community Center & School

A home that belongs to all of us

Our Armenian school has served families across greater Seattle for decades. It has outgrown its space, and our weddings, concerts and celebrations happen in rented halls. Together, we're building a new two-story Community Center and School right beside our church in Redmond.

What we're building

Two floors: one to learn, one to celebrate

The preliminary plans put the school downstairs and a banquet and concert hall upstairs, so classes, clubs and family milestones each have room to grow.

First floor

School and clubs

  • Four classrooms sized for 130 students
  • A gym for sports and assemblies
  • Club rooms for chess, arts and crafts, and computers
  • A welcoming lobby, storage and utilities

Second floor

Banquet and concert hall

  • A 3,815 sq ft hall for up to 250 guests
  • A stage and dance floor for concerts, weddings and holidays
  • A commercial kitchen to cook for a full house
  • An outdoor deck, cloakroom and storage

Where it goes

West of the church, on our own campus

The site was chosen because it keeps costs down and school running during construction:

  • An existing Conditional Use Permit simplifies pre-construction
  • It avoids costly Redmond-Woodinville Road improvements
  • It connects easily to the church's sewer line
  • Classes carry on without disruption while we build

Plus church, school and a loan

Volunteers cut the cost

Community members have already pledged HVAC and general contractor services. Every donated skill is money saved.

Then it pays its own way

After year three, renting the hall, kitchen and classrooms on days we aren't using them keeps the center self-supporting.
