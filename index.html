The campaign

Our community has outgrown its space

For decades our community has passed the Armenian language and culture on to its children. Today they learn in the rooms of an old house, and our weddings, concerts and holidays happen in rented halls.

Together, we're building a two-story Community Center right beside our church in Redmond: learning spaces for 130 people, a 250-guest hall with a stage, and a commercial kitchen. Children will learn the Armenian language here, along with other languages spoken across the Redmond community.

The plan is simple: 200 Founding Families giving $100 a month for three years. After that, renting the hall, kitchen and learning rooms on days we aren't using them keeps the center self-supporting.

What we're building

Two floors: one to learn, one to celebrate

The preliminary plans put learning spaces downstairs and a banquet and concert hall upstairs, so language classes, clubs and family milestones each have room to grow.

First floor

Learning and clubs

  • Learning rooms for up to 130 people at once
  • Language classes for children in Armenian and other languages spoken across Redmond
  • Club rooms for chess, arts and crafts, music and computers
  • A gym for sports and gatherings, plus a lobby and storage

Second floor

Banquet and concert hall

  • A hall of about 3,800 sq ft for up to 250 guests
  • A stage and dance floor for concerts, weddings and holidays
  • A commercial kitchen to cook for a full house
  • An outdoor deck, cloakroom and storage

Where it goes

West of the church, on our own campus

  • Permits are simplerAn existing Conditional Use Permit eases pre-construction
  • Lower road costsIt avoids costly Redmond-Woodinville Road improvements
  • Easy utilitiesIt connects easily to the church's sewer line
  • No disruptionCurrent programs carry on while we build

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes, to the extent allowed by law. Gifts are made to the Armenian Apostolic Church of Seattle Community Fund, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 91-1189180.

Why monthly, and why three years?

Steady monthly gifts give the project predictable funding for planning, permits and construction. Three years covers design, building and opening. After that, rental income from the hall, kitchen and learning rooms is planned to keep the center self-supporting.

What will children learn here?

The first floor is planned around learning: language classes for children in Armenian and in other languages spoken across the Redmond community, clubs for chess, arts and crafts, music and computers, and a gym for sports.

How much will the project cost?

Rough estimates are $2.5 to $3 million. An accurate figure will be available once the detailed design is finalized.

How long will it take to build?

If there are no delays, about two years from start to finish: the first year for design documents and permits, the second for construction.

Can I give by check or through my employer?

Yes. The donation page has the mailing address for checks and how to find us on Benevity and other workplace giving portals.

Who do I ask if I have questions?

Email the fundraising committee at armenianswa@gmail.com. We're happy to help.
